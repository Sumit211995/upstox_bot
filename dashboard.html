How this bot works (and its limits)

1 · Data

Index prices and India VIX come free from Upstox's public API (5-minute candles since Jan 2022, live during market hours). No login, no key.

2 · Option prices

Live option prices need a broker login, so premiums are estimated from the index, time to expiry and India VIX (Black-Scholes on a trading-hours clock). Checked against real NSE closing prices: typical error ≈ 9%.

3 · Rules

Each "book" is one strategy on one index with settings chosen on 2022–2024 and checked on 2025–2026. The paper bot replays the exact same simulator on today's live candles.

4 · Costs

Every trade pays ₹20 brokerage per order, STT 0.1% on sells, exchange + SEBI charges, stamp duty, 18% GST and 0.5% slippage per order.

Paper results are estimates. Option selling has rare large losses (gaps, events); hedges (wings) cap them. About 9 in 10 retail F&O traders lose money (SEBI). Not financial advice.
